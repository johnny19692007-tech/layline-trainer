Optional: melody for "Fifteen Men on the Dead Man's Chest"

Put the public-domain 1891 score of "A Piratical Ballad" (music by Henry Waller,
words by Young E. Allison) in this folder, named exactly:

    PiraticalBallad.mid

Download it free from Project Gutenberg:
https://www.gutenberg.org/files/19273/19273-h/music/PiraticalBallad.mid

Once it's here and uploaded with the rest of the app, the song joins the playlist.
Without it, the app still shows the lyrics in the Lyrics panel.
